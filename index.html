the problem

eight people, one bill, one person squinting at a receipt doing arithmetic while the food goes cold. someone had the biryani, someone had a salad, someone only had chai, and everyone ends up paying the same because working out the truth takes longer than it's worth.

splitting evenly is a tax on whoever ordered least.

how it goes

  1. 1 · snap it

    point at the receipt. the lines and the tax fill themselves in. you check them — one wrong digit is a real argument later, so hissa always asks.

  2. 2 · pass the code

    four letters. read them across the table or send the link. no account, no install, nobody downloads anything. only three or four of you? pass the phone round instead — everyone takes a turn on one screen.

  3. 3 · everyone taps

    tap what you ate. shared a plate? tap it twice. your number sits at the bottom of the screen and updates as people go. then send the result to the group chat, as text or as a picture.

what it does about the awkward bits

claims are portions, not people. a dish for two takes two taps and costs you two shares of it.

gst, service, discount and tip ride along proportionally. you never pay tip on someone else's steak. discount before tax or after? hissa checks the arithmetic against the printed total and picks the one that matches.

every line is distributed to the last paisa. the group can't under-cover a bill by accident.

the naan nobody admits to. flip share leftovers and it splits across everyone who ate.
